The world

A colony that spins so its people have weight

The Spinhold is a cylinder kilometres long, turning in deep space. Down its axis runs the Sunline, the light that is its sun. The city on its hull is where you fly, one ring at a time.

The Spinhold

Grown cell by cell by builder machines, generations ago. That is why the whole world is voxels: everything here was made from the same cubes.

The Lattice

The builders have woken up wrong. They still follow their oldest order, build the Spinhold, but they have lost the plan, so they break it down and rebuild it as more of themselves.

The Holders

The colony’s people: hull crews, gunners, medics and engineers with no ship of their own. The Lattice carries them off because it thinks they are part of the hull that came loose.

Work in progress

The only warm thing in a cold neon world

Shoot a Gleaner and the Holder it was carrying falls back to the hull. Catch them, fly them through the Lift, and the colony’s core keeps them safe. Nothing grim is ever shown: a Holder the Lattice takes simply flickers out.

Know the Lattice

Six machines, one colour each

Every kind of Lattice glows in a colour of its own, so a crowded ring still reads at a glance. Each one is made of cubes, and each one breaks back into them.

  • Mote

    The small swarm. Twenty at a time, chasing you round the ring.

  • Lancer

    Keeps its distance and fires slow, aimed shots.

  • Spur

    Grows out of the hull and fires straight up.

  • Diver

    Hovers, draws a line to you, then dives straight down it.

  • Sower

    Crosses the ring without turning, dropping seeds that stay until shot.

  • Gleaner

    Rises from the hull carrying a Holder. Reach the top and it is gone with them.

…and a Foreman at the end of every ring

Its core shrugs off every hit until its weak points fall. Tap a card to see who runs each ring.

The tender

A hull-maintenance craft, armed in a hurry

Tenders used to fly the hull fixing what the Lattice built. Yours is the last one flying, and it has three tricks for a crowded ring.

  • Dash

    Hold it and the tender tears through the swarm untouchable, killing whatever it hits, until the meter runs dry.

  • Pulse

    A shockwave sent both ways round the whole ring, clearing every machine and every shot it meets.

  • Sunline burst

    Kills charge it. Full, the tender draws on the colony’s sun and fires it ahead for four slowed seconds.

Keep the streak alive

Every kill raises the multiplier a step, all the way to ×99, and refills its window. Let two and a half seconds pass without one, or lose the tender, and it falls back to one.

Every rescue pays

Each delivery through the Lift pays the next thing on the list: a life, a pulse, a shield, then points that grow with every Holder you bring in at once. Save everyone in a ring and it pays a bonus.
